# Image scene patterns

Choose one primary pattern that fits the destination. User-provided facts and brand assets take precedence.

## Product and commerce

- Studio catalog: accurate structure, neutral or brand-color backdrop, controlled soft light, and a grounded shadow without unrelated props.
- Lifestyle: a credible environment and use case with plausible hands, scale, and physical interaction.
- Material close-up: tight framing around texture, mechanics, or surface detail while preserving enough context to identify the product.
- Hero banner: place the product to one side and leave space for text to be added later.
- Concept visual: suspension, liquid, miniature scenes, or CGI-like materials are allowed, but the real product structure and label must remain accurate.

## Advertising and social

- Static ad: one hook, one subject, one piece of visual evidence, and a clear copy-safe area.
- UGC still: a credible phone-camera angle, natural environment, and realistic handling without fabricated testimonials.
- Carousel: establish consistent color, lens, light, margins, and product scale, then give each frame one distinct communication task.
- Campaign key visual: a memorable silhouette, a deliberate color mechanism, one dominant metaphor, and a simplified background.

## People, covers, and posters

- Portrait: define framing, expression, gaze, wardrobe, background, key/fill/rim light, and natural facial structure.
- Identity reference: preserve facial structure, skin tone, hairline, apparent age, and distinguishing features.
- Vertical cover: keep the key action in the upper focal area and reserve lower interface-safe space.
- Landscape thumbnail: one dominant face or object, strong subject separation, little clutter, and readability at small size.
- Poster: one visual proposition, deliberate hierarchy, clean copy space, and controlled color.

When the user asks only for creative directions, offer distinct options such as product detail, everyday use, or visual metaphor without starting generation. If the user also asks you to choose a direction and generate it, proceed within that request. Offering several ideas does not authorize several billable tasks.
